## Framework Overview

The core thesis of the Anchoring Framework: any wave packet — from galaxies to watersheds, cosmos to microscopic time series — resonates with its ambient background. The environmental structure defines the coherence of the wave packet: its capacity to resist perturbations and sustain stable configuration.

Three core state variables:

| Symbol | Meaning | Application |
|---|---|---|
| **S** (Survival Factor) | Coherence time ÷ Perturbation period | The wave packet’s robustness against perturbation |
| **Gₐₙcₕₒᵣ** (Anchoring Stiffness) | Background frequency ÷ Wave density × S | Environmental constraint strength |
| **R** (Resonance Overlap) | Spectral overlap between local system and background | Degree of environmental matching |

The framework manifests cross-scale:
- **Cosmic scale**: Galaxy rotation curves arise from anchoring stiffness, no dark matter required
- **Hydrological scale**: Seasonal runoff architecture in watersheds is governed by snow anchor stability
- **Microscopic neural scale**: EEG limit-cycle crystallization (Taixu-Engine). Neural coherence and state transitions are quantified by S and Gₐₙcₕₒᵣ.

## Empirical Cases

### Case 1: Galaxy Rotation Curves — Single-Layer Anchoring (Cosmic Scale)
Fitting 98 SPARC galaxies under the Anchoring Framework yields a median residual of 9.14 km/s, **without invoking dark matter**. The α parameter (density sensitivity of survival factor) correlates significantly with galaxy gas fraction (r² = 0.439).

### Case 2: Galaxy Rotation Curves — Two-Layer Anchoring and Mass-Scale Coupling (Cosmic Scale)
Analysis of 159 SPARC galaxies reveals:
- **Stellar disks follow Newtonian gravity** (k_star = 0.005 ± 0.005 /kpc, consistent with null)
- **Gas disks depart from Newtonian gravity** (k_gas = 0.244 ± 0.022 /kpc, characteristic length 4.1 kpc)
- Gas-layer anchoring is controlled by two independent variables: gas mass fraction w_gas and HI disk radius R_HI
- Double-variable model: k = 0.127·w_gas + 0.530/R_HI, 5-fold CV R² = 0.62
- Independent validation on THINGS survey (14 overlapping galaxies): massive galaxies show consistent k (difference < 0.03)

This provides a dark-matter-free interpretation of flat rotation curves, and is observationally distinguishable from MOND because MOND does not separate stellar/gas components or distribution scales.

### Case 3: Altai West Sub-basin (Hydrological Scale, High Sensitivity)
- Location: 86.0–87.5°E, 47.0–48.2°N
- SRF (Spring Runoff Fraction): 0.384
- SEI trend: -0.0092/yr (58-year cumulative: -37.6%)
- Snow depth trend: -0.058 cm/yr
- Conclusion: Triple consistent signal; the system is rapidly shifting toward rain-dominated anchoring.

### Case 4: Hutubi River (Hydrological Scale, Low Sensitivity)
- Location: 86.05–87.08°E, 43.07–45.20°N
- SRF: 0.377
- SEI trend: -0.0019/yr (58-year cumulative: -6.5%)
- Snow depth trend: +0.009 cm/yr (stable)
- Conclusion: Weak directional signal; glacier buffering likely suppresses seasonal extremization.
